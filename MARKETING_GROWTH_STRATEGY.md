## Growth Hacks

### 1. Viral Loops

**Invite-Only Beta**:
- Limited access creates FOMO
- Users get 3 invites
- Invites become valuable

**Social Proof**:
- "10,000 people renting on NeighbourFriendly"
- "Join India's fastest-growing rental community"
- Real-time booking notifications

---

### 2. Gamification

**User Levels**:
- Bronze: 0-5 transactions
- Silver: 6-20 transactions
- Gold: 21-50 transactions
- Platinum: 51+ transactions

**Benefits**:
- Higher levels = lower fees
- Priority support
- Featured listings
- Exclusive deals
