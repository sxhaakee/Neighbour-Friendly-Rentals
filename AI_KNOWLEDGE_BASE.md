## What We Do

NeighbourFriendly Rentals is a community-driven platform that connects neighbors to rent items, services, and properties locally. We believe in building stronger communities through resource sharing.

### Three Main Categories:

#### 1. **Items** 📦
Rent physical goods from your neighbors:
- Electronics (cameras, laptops, speakers, microphones)
- Tools & Equipment (drills, ladders, power tools)
- Sports & Fitness (bicycles, gym equipment, camping gear)
- Musical Instruments (guitars, keyboards, drums)
- Furniture (tables, chairs, sofas)
- Books & Media
- Clothing & Accessories
- Party & Event supplies
- Gaming & Consoles
- Outdoor & Camping gear
- Baby & Kids items

#### 2. **Services** 🛠️
Hire local service providers:
- Photography & Videography
- Catering & Food services
- Event Planning & Management
- Music & Entertainment (DJs, bands)
- Home Services (plumbing, electrical, repairs)
- Beauty & Wellness (makeup artists, stylists)
- Professional services

#### 3. **Properties** 🏠
Rent residential and commercial spaces:
- Apartments
- Houses & Villas
- Rooms (PG/Hostel)
- Commercial spaces
- Short-term and long-term rentals

## How It Works

### For Renters:
1. **Browse:** Search for items, services, or properties
2. **Select:** Choose what you need
3. **Book:** Request booking with dates
4. **Connect:** Message owner for details
5. **Meet:** Pick up item or receive service
6. **Return:** Return item on time
7. **Review:** Rate your experience

### For Owners:
1. **List:** Add your item/service/property
2. **Wait:** Admin reviews (usually 24-48 hours)
3. **Approved:** Listing goes live
4. **Receive:** Get booking requests
5. **Accept:** Confirm bookings
6. **Deliver:** Provide item/service
7. **Earn:** Receive payment
8. **Review:** Rate the renter

## Common Questions & Answers

### Q: Is it safe to rent from strangers?
**A:** Yes! We have ID verification, user ratings, reviews, and admin moderation. Plus, you're renting from neighbors, not strangers.

### Q: What if an item gets damaged?
**A:** Renters are responsible for damages. We recommend both parties take photos before/after. Insurance is recommended for high-value items.

### Q: How do payments work?
**A:** Currently, users arrange payments directly. We're integrating Razorpay for secure in-app payments soon.

### Q: How long does approval take?
**A:** Admin reviews listings within 24-48 hours to ensure quality and safety.

### Q: Can I cancel a booking?
**A:** Yes, but cancellation policies vary by owner. Check the listing details.

### Q: What's the commission rate?
**A:** 5-15% depending on category. Premium members get reduced rates.

### Q: Do I need to verify my ID?
**A:** Not mandatory, but highly recommended. Verified users get more bookings and trust.

### Q: Can I list multiple items?
**A:** Yes! Free users have limits, Premium members get unlimited listings.

### Q: What if someone doesn't return my item?
**A:** Report to admin immediately. We'll help mediate. Consider police report for theft.

### Q: How do I become Premium?
**A:** Go to Profile → Upgrade to Premium → Choose plan → Pay ₹99/month or ₹999/year.
